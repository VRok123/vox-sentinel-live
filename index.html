Audio Engine: DSP & Convolutional Neural Networks

Our audio detection relies on a localized TensorFlow Lite CNN operating in tandem with algorithmic Digital Signal Processing (DSP). Raw `.wav` waveforms are evaluated dynamically across multiple vectors.

Feature Extraction Math

1. Fast Fourier Transformation (FFT)

We apply the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) to map time-domain waveforms into frequency-domain spectrograms.

$$ X(m, \omega) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x(n) w(n - m) e^{-j\omega n} $$ Cooley & Tukey (1965): Machine Calculation of Complex Fourier Series

Spectral Leakage & Windowing

Raw FFT assumes infinite periodicity. To prevent "spectral leakage" across frequency bins, VoxSentinel applies a Hann window function, mathematically tapering the edges of the audio frame to zero before transformation.

2. Mel Scale Transformation

Frequencies ($f$) are non-linearly compressed to the Mel scale ($m$) to mimic the logarithmic acoustic perception of the human cochlea.

$$ m = 2595 \log_{10}\left(1 + \frac{f}{700}\right) $$ Stevens et al. (1937): A Scale for the Measurement of Pitch
Click for Deep Dive
Triangular Filter Banks

The transformation utilizes 128 overlapping triangular filters. Below 1kHz, filters are linearly spaced to capture human speech formants; above 1kHz, they space logarithmically to compress and discard irrelevant high-frequency noise.

Return
3. Power to Decibels ($dB$)

Raw amplitude spectrograms are converted to logarithmic power spectrograms relative to peak power ($S_{ref}$) for CNN ingestion.

$$ L_{dB} = 10 \log_{10} \left( \frac{S}{S_{ref}} \right) $$
Click for Deep Dive
Dynamic Range Compression

Raw power values span massive magnitudes, which causes Vanishing Gradients during neural backpropagation. The $10\log_{10}$ conversion strictly bounds the data into a dense, CNN-friendly tensor matrix.

Heuristics & Classification

4. Pitch Variance ($\sigma_{F_0}$)

Using Probabilistic YIN (pYIN), we extract the fundamental frequency ($F_0$). Low variance flags monotone AI delivery lacking human prosody.

$$ \sigma_{F_0} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum (F_{0,i} - \mu_{F_0})^2} $$ Mauch & Dixon (2014): pYIN Fundamental Frequency Estimator
Click for Deep Dive
Jitter & Micro-Intonation

While AI can mimic macro-pitch changes, it fails at "Jitter"—the cycle-to-cycle frequency variations caused by the chaotic physics of human vocal cords. Low variance mathematically isolates vocoder synthesis.

Return
5. Silence Ratio & Spectral Roll-Off

We measure the ratio of speaking time to silence, and analyze spectral energy above 7kHz to catch missing high-frequency data caused by AI vocoders.

$$ Ratio_{>7kHz} = \frac{\sum_{f>7000} |S(f)|^2}{\sum |S(f)|^2} $$

Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR)

Beyond absolute volume thresholds, the engine measures the rate at which the signal changes sign. AI audio often leaves unnatural, continuous white noise in the upper frequency bands during "silent" spans.

Return
6. CNN Sigmoid Activation

The 128x128 Mel-spectrogram is processed by the CNN. The final classification layer uses a Sigmoid function to output a normalized probability ($0 \to 1$).

$$ \sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}} $$ Zhang et al. (2021): Deep Learning for Audio Deepfake Detection

Binary Cross-Entropy

Sigmoid maps raw logits to a strict $(0, 1)$ range. Unlike Softmax (which outputs a distribution across multiple classes), Sigmoid forces the CNN to weigh evidence strictly on a continuous spectrum between Real and Fake.
