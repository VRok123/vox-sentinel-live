Audio Engine: DSP & Convolutional Neural Networks

Our audio detection relies on a localized TensorFlow Lite CNN operating in tandem with algorithmic Digital Signal Processing (DSP). Raw `.wav` waveforms are evaluated dynamically across multiple vectors.

Feature Extraction Math

1. Fast Fourier Transformation (FFT)

We apply the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) to map time-domain waveforms into frequency-domain spectrograms.

$$ X(m, \omega) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x(n) w(n - m) e^{-j\omega n} $$ Cooley & Tukey (1965): Machine Calculation of Complex Fourier Series 2. Mel Scale Transformation

Frequencies ($f$) are non-linearly compressed to the Mel scale ($m$) to mimic the logarithmic acoustic perception of the human cochlea.

$$ m = 2595 \log_{10}\left(1 + \frac{f}{700}\right) $$ Stevens et al. (1937): A Scale for the Measurement of Pitch 3. Power to Decibels ($dB$)

Raw amplitude spectrograms are converted to logarithmic power spectrograms relative to peak power ($S_{ref}$) for CNN ingestion.

$$ L_{dB} = 10 \log_{10} \left( \frac{S}{S_{ref}} \right) $$

Heuristics & Classification

4. Pitch Variance ($\sigma_{F_0}$)

Using Probabilistic YIN (pYIN), we extract the fundamental frequency ($F_0$). Low variance flags monotone AI delivery lacking human prosody.

$$ \sigma_{F_0} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum (F_{0,i} - \mu_{F_0})^2} $$ Mauch & Dixon (2014): pYIN Fundamental Frequency Estimator 5. Silence Ratio & Spectral Roll-Off

We measure the ratio of speaking time to silence, and analyze spectral energy above 7kHz to catch missing high-frequency data caused by AI vocoders.

$$ Ratio_{>7kHz} = \frac{\sum_{f>7000} |S(f)|^2}{\sum |S(f)|^2} $$ 6. CNN Sigmoid Activation

The 128x128 Mel-spectrogram is processed by the CNN. The final classification layer uses a Sigmoid function to output a normalized probability ($0 \to 1$).

$$ \sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}} $$ Zhang et al. (2021): Deep Learning for Audio Deepfake Detection

Text Engine: NLP Math & RoBERTa

Our text detection runs locally on a custom-trained RoBERTa classification head. It evaluates both statistical properties (Stylometrics) and deep syntactic probability to detect LLM generation patterns.

Statistical Heuristics

1. Burstiness ($\sigma$)

We calculate the standard deviation of sentence lengths ($L_i$) across the text. AI models generate predictable, uniform sentences (low variance), while human emotion creates chaotic sentence length distributions.

$$ \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (L_i - \mu)^2} $$ Wang et al. (2023): Robust Deepfake Text Detection (Stylometrics) 2. Shannon Entropy / Perplexity ($H$)

We evaluate the unpredictability of token sequences. Lower entropy implies text generated by a highly constrained probabilistic model (LLM) rather than a human vocabulary.

$$ H(X) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} P(x_i) \log_2 P(x_i) $$ Gehrmann et al. (2019): GLTR - Statistical Detection of Generated Text

Neural Engine (RoBERTa)

3. Scaled Dot-Product Attention

Fine-tuned on the HC3 dataset, our RoBERTa model utilizes bidirectional self-attention to map the deep contextual relationships between words, identifying syntactic "plasticity" common in ChatGPT outputs.

$$ Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V $$ Vaswani et al. (2017): Attention Is All You Need Liu et al. (2019): RoBERTa: A Robustly Optimized BERT Guo et al. (2023): Training Corpus (HC3 Dataset)

Vision Engine: Hybrid Cloud Temporal Analysis

To bypass strict EC2 instance memory limits, VoxSentinel pairs local extraction logic with a scalable Cloud Vision API for deepfake video and image detection. The system focuses on physical logic rather than pixel-level noise.

Local Extraction (Metadata)

Software Signature Sweeping: The Python backend parses raw EXIF headers before rendering, flagging hidden metadata injected by generators (e.g., Software: Midjourney).

Cloud Analysis (Temporal & Kinematics)

Structural Shifting: Analyzes complex background geometry across multiple frames to detect the slow, non-Euclidean "morphing" characteristic of generative models.

Kinematic Failures: Evaluates shadow angles, reflection consistency, and gravity mapping.
